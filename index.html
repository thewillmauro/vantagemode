Guardrails
SAFE TO SCALE

Guardrails are what make automation safe to run at volume. They live in the system itself, so they hold even when nobody is watching.

01
No cold outreach to consumers

Outbound targets businesses only. Individuals hear from you after they opt in, never before.

Enforced by: Targeting rules
02
No sensitive records as leads

Court filings, medical data, and similar records stay off-limits, even when they are technically public.

Enforced by: Source allowlist
03
Permitted data only

Every data source is checked against its terms of use before it enters the pipeline.

Enforced by: Source allowlist
04
A person approves every send

The system drafts and a person reviews and sends. Nothing goes out on autopilot.

Enforced by: Review step
05
Accurate claims only

No guaranteed outcomes and no invented stats. Every message is checked for accuracy before it goes out.

Enforced by: Review step
06
Compliance built in

Unsubscribe links, a physical address, and a suppression list checked before every send. Separate sending domains protect your main one.

Enforced by: Sequencer + suppression list
07
Decisions on record

Every tradeoff is written down, so your team knows why the system works the way it does.

Enforced by: Decision records
08
Calls follow consent rules

Recording disclosures where state law requires them, calling hours honored, and no automated calls or texts to mobile numbers without consent.

Enforced by: Dialer settings

Got Questions

FAQ

Everything you need to know before we start working together.

Ask Directly →
What does a GTM engineer actually do?
+
GTM stands for go-to-market: everything that turns a stranger into a customer. A GTM engineer builds the systems your sales and marketing teams run on: where account data comes from, how it gets enriched and researched, how leads are scored and routed, how records sync to your CRM, and how outreach and reporting work. Think of it as software engineering pointed at pipeline instead of product.
Will this replace our SDRs?
+
No. A sales development rep (SDR) finds and qualifies new prospects so your closers have meetings to run. The system takes over the research, list building, and first drafts, so your SDRs spend their time talking with real buyers. Many teams use it to get more from the SDRs they have before hiring the next one.
Do you work in our existing stack?
+
Yes. We work in the tools you already pay for, whatever they are, and only add something new when it clearly earns its place. The process does not depend on any one tool.
Will you send email on our behalf?
+
By default, no. We build the drafting, review, and sending infrastructure, and a person on your team approves what goes out. Sending domains are kept separate from your main domain, suppression lists are honored, and every message stays CAN-SPAM compliant.
How do you keep AI research accurate?
+
Agents have to cite the page where they found each answer, and they are graded against a hand-labeled set of your real accounts before they run on the full list. Anything uncertain goes to a human review step instead of straight into your CRM.
Do you still produce video?
+
Yes, as part of go-to-market. Launch films, ad creative, founder content, and product demos are produced in house, so the creative and the pipeline it runs through are planned together.
How does pricing work?
+
Audits and builds are fixed-scope and fixed-price, whether you start with one piece or the full pipeline. Fractional work and upkeep are monthly retainers. Tools like your data provider and sequencer run on your own accounts, and you pay those vendors directly. After a discovery call, we send a proposal with clear deliverables, a timeline, and a price. No surprises.

What do you do with our call notes?
+
We usually work from the notes your AI notetaker or dialer already writes, not the raw recordings. The system pulls out the fields your teams care about, like pains, objections, competitors, feature requests, and next steps, and writes them to your CRM. Recording consent stays with your existing process, personal details are redacted before anything syncs, and access follows your CRM permissions.
How do you prove it is working?
+
We baseline your numbers before we build anything, then track the metrics each piece of the system is meant to move: pipeline created, speed to lead, reply and meeting rates, conversion, and cost per qualified account. You get a scoreboard and a regular review, and every change is tested against that baseline, so the numbers decide what we do next.
